AI RESEARCH

Connect-4 AI Tutor

Developed an AI tutoring system as part of a research project at San José State University investigating how different levels of AI assistance affect learning.

Built three tutoring models with varying levels of instructional guidance and tested them with beginner Connect-4 players. The system incorporated Google's Gemini LLM and retrieval-augmented generation to provide strategic guidance.

We found that providing more AI instruction did not necessarily improve learning, suggesting that over-instruction can interfere with independent problem-solving.

Python Tkinter Gemini API RAG AI Data Analysis
